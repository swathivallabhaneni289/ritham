Phase 1 · Onboarding & Safety Intake — sketch 002 (round 2)

Athletic, premium, unmistakably fitness.

Round 1 (sketch 001 — Ember Horizon / Quiet Confidence / Stone & Sage) was rejected: too basic, too Duolingo, too wellness-coded. This round changes structure, not just color — different radii, different type mechanism, different Momentum shape per direction — grounded in Nike Training Club / WHOOP / Peloton and current 2026 mobile trends.

Hard rule across all three: the accent is a signal (CTA / live data / progress), never a decorative surface wash. No rounded numerals, no ring/badge/bloom-shaped progress.

Update: orange is out. Use the Accent swatches above each phone to try Blue, Red, or Violet on any direction — live, no rebuild needed.

A · Afterburn

instrument-panel premium

Mechanism
Near-black substrate (WHOOP/Peloton "Woodsmoke" family), flat opaque cards, one saturated amber signal reserved for CTA/live data/progress. Liquid Glass only on the floating Start-Session capsule.
Momentum
3-slot bar strip, hairline outline → solid amber fill on log, current slot pulses. No ring, no badge.
Momo
Bounded, warm-lit portrait card — never floated loose over live data.
Risk to watch
Hardest case for a painted character to sit on a flat dark panel — worth stress-testing specifically. Also: dark-only isn't defensible for this audience (see Peloton's Oct 2025 reversal) — Hard Court is the equally-finished light mode this direction needs alongside it, not a fallback.

B · Full Stride

editorial / photographic premium

Mechanism
Full-bleed imagery (placeholder here — real hero photography/renders needed) with a bottom scrim, massive condensed headline type, amber as solid hard-edged shapes on top of the image, never a filter over it.
Momentum
3-slot filmstrip, amber-filled per logged session, ties the data form to the imagery language instead of a chart.
Momo
Most load-bearing of the three — full campaign-photography treatment, lowest mascot-collision risk of the three directions.
Risk to watch
Imagery must deliberately span ages, not just 20-something athletes, or this re-imports the youth-coded risk on its own. Every overlaid-text moment needs a real contrast QA pass per image. The Safety Screening screen (right) deliberately drops the photography — no hero imagery on that surface, same rule as Momo being absent there in round 1.

C · Hard Court

graphic / structural premium, light mode

Mechanism
Flat near-white ground, hard-edged solid amber color-blocks (not gradients or tints), scoreboard-tile numerals, ALL-CAPS tracked micro-labels. Hierarchy comes from weight contrast, not color.
Momentum
3-slot rectangular block grid — a scoreboard tally, not a ring or bloom.
Momo
Bounded illustrated tile, like a trading card — reads as the one deliberate warm/organic element inside an otherwise geometric grid.
Why this one matters
This is the real, equally-finished light mode — Peloton shipped dark-only in Oct 2025 and reversed within 4 weeks after backlash. Whichever dark direction ships, this can't be an afterthought toggle.
