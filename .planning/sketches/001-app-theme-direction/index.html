Which world does Momo live in?

Three grounded directions for Ritham's app-wide background, built from a research pass on Calm, Headspace, Apple Fitness+/Health, and AllTrails/Strava — not from taste alone. Each phone below has its own Welcome / Safety Screening toggle; the screening screen stays flat and Momo-free in all three by deliberate design.

A · Ember Horizon

Soft cream→apricot daylight gradient. Amber is reserved for Momentum/live accents only — never a button, never a ring. Momo lives in the gradient; the screening screen goes flat.

Borrowed from
Calm's adjacent-hue gradient technique, re-keyed to morning light instead of night sky (energizing, not sleep-first).
Momentum shape
Open arc (270°) — deliberately not a full ring, to avoid reading as an unofficial 4th Apple Activity ring.
Risk to watch
Momo's painted volume needs to share the gradient's lighting logic, or he'll look pasted on top of a flat color block.

B · Quiet Confidence

Near-colorless, Apple-native ground. Warmth comes almost entirely from rounded numerals; amber is a single sparing accent, never a scene. Momo shrinks to a small glyph.

Borrowed from
Apple Health/Fitness+'s "Deference" principle — chrome recedes, your own numbers lead. SF Pro Rounded numerals are the one warmth lever.
Momentum shape
Dot-progression row — simplest, most system-native reading of progress.
Risk to watch
Lowest-risk direction, but worth checking it doesn't feel too restrained for a brand that's already invested in a painted mascot.

C · Stone & Sage

Earthy, tactile field — cream, sage, forest green — with a subtle organic surface texture instead of a flat fill. Amber spent sparingly, gated strictly to "live right now."

Borrowed from
AllTrails' cream/sage restraint and organic surface texture, without its athletic/aspirational photography (wrong for a screening gate).
Momentum shape
A bloom — petals accumulate as sessions log, echoing growth-only, never-decaying framing.
Risk to watch
Most tactile, least "app-like" of the three — worth checking texture reads as warm, not just busy, at small sizes.
