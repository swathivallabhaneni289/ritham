<objective>
Build the Kid Ideas screen and its entry point: one combined screen (D-05) showing the food ideas
and the movement ideas as a browsable library the parent can reopen any time (D-08), read-only with
no favourite, tried, or saved state of any kind (D-09), reached from a new Settings row rather than
a dashboard section (D-04), carrying an optional multi-child section (D-07) scoped to the single
preteen band with no age input at all (D-06).

Purpose: this is the plan where every earlier artifact becomes something a person can actually use,
and it is the plan most at risk from reflex. Three reflexes to resist, each named in
04.2-RESEARCH.md: adding a `HomeHubView` dashboard section because that is the most recently
reinforced pattern in this codebase (Pitfall 1); copying `DietPlanView(flow:)`'s signature even
though this screen writes no screening data and needs no `OnboardingFlow` (Pattern 1); and adding a
required name field because that is how most apps model a person (Pitfall 2).

Output: `ChildEntryStore.swift`, `KidIdeasView.swift`, a Settings row and sheet, and
`KidIdeasTests.swift`.
</objective>

    identifier no longer matches, mirroring `PrivacyZoneStore`.
