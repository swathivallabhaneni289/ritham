<objective>
Build the screening questionnaire: the opening disclaimer, the seven gate questions with their two
medication follow-ups, both clearance interstitials, the nine-category condition checklist, the severity
follow-ups, the eating-pattern follow-up, and the universal follow-up.

Purpose: this is the sequence HEALTH-01 specifies and the input to the escalation engine already built
and tested in plan 01-06. The screens' job is to collect fixed choices faithfully and to render
HEALTH-05's copy blocks at their defined touchpoints; every safety decision is made in core, not here.

Two content constraints govern throughout. HEALTH-01 forbids free text and live generated advice
anywhere in this flow, which the shared question component already enforces by having no text-entry
mode. And `docs/health-screening.md` §1.5 requires that the eating-pattern screen never show the user a
score, a positive-or-negative result, or a diagnostic label — the result surfaces only as a neutral,
supportive message about what changed and why.

Output: Seven registered screening screens composed from the shared components and the copy catalog.
</objective>
