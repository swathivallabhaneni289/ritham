- It is not on the default search path. `swift test` succeeds only when given, in this order:
  `-Xswiftc -F -Xswiftc <FW>` then `-Xlinker -F -Xlinker <FW>` then
  `-Xlinker -rpath -Xlinker <FW>` then `-Xlinker -rpath -Xlinker <LIB>`
  where FW = `<dev-dir>/Library/Developer/Frameworks`
  and   LIB = `<dev-dir>/Library/Developer/usr/lib`   (this second rpath resolves
  `lib_TestingInterop.dylib`, which lives outside the framework bundle)
- `Package.swift` MUST declare a macOS platform floor of at least `.macOS(.v14)`. Without it the
  `@Test` macro expansion fails to compile with "'isolation()' is only available in macOS 10.15 or newer".
- `--filter <name>` passes through the wrapper correctly (verified).
