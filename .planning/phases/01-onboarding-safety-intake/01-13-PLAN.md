<objective>
Build the five screens that open the app: welcome, the explanation-register choice, age, dietary
pattern, and the privacy explainer.

Purpose: these carry three requirements whose promises are made in copy and must be kept in behaviour.
The age screen's helper tells the user we ask "never to sort you into a different app", so it must not
lead anywhere structurally different — it resolves a tier and hands back to the router. The dietary
pattern helper says the choice "only affects example foods later — never your health screening", so it
must be stored on the profile and never reach gate resolution. The privacy screen says nothing is shared
or synced by default, so this phase must in fact request nothing and sync nothing.

Output: Five registered step views composed from the shared components, with the age validation tested.
</objective>
