<objective>
Build small, closed, invite-only groups and the membership predicate every later group-scoped
surface authorizes against.

Purpose: GROUPEVENTS-01 requires groups with no public or joinable-by-anyone tier at all — not a
private default with a public option, but no such tier existing. `docs/group-events.md` §1 is
explicit that a group is invisible to search, not listed on any profile, and generates no public
or aggregate signal, and that membership mechanics are deliberately unlike Strava's: any member
leaves at any time with no ownership handoff, and the organizer leaving simply continues the group
because there is nothing public attached to the role.

This plan also exports the single membership check the feed, events, and completion surfaces all
sit behind. 04.1-RESEARCH.md's Security Domain names access control as the highest-relevance
category for this phase, and its recommended shape is a membership-scoped query rather than a bare
fetch by id — so the predicate lives here, once.

Output: the `internal/groups` package, migration `0004_groups`, and eight authenticated routes.
</objective>

  <behavior>
    - Any member can leave; afterwards they are not in the member list and the group still exists.
    - The organizer leaving removes only their membership: the group remains, every other member
      keeps access, and no other member is promoted to organizer.
    - Leaving with the keep disposition leaves the caller's past completions visible to the group.
    - Leaving with the remove disposition hides the caller's past completions from the group's
      feed, and the two dispositions are distinguishable afterwards.
    - The last member leaving is permitted; nothing blocks it and no transfer is demanded.
    - Under the any-member policy, any member can remove any other member.
    - Under the organizer-only policy, a non-organizer's removal attempt fails with the
      not-permitted sentinel.
    - Nobody can remove themselves through the removal route; leaving is the route for that.
    - Only a member can change the removal policy.
    - The membership gate returns nil for a member and the not-a-member sentinel for everyone else,
      including for a group id that does not exist.
  </behavior>

| T-04.1-44 | Elevation of Privilege | joining or reading a group by guessing or obtaining its id | critical | mitigate | Opaque UUID ids plus membership-scoped queries on every read and write; `RequireMember` is the single gate and joining additionally requires a pending invitation. | T-04.1-47 | Elevation of Privilege | removing a member without authority | medium | mitigate | Removal is gated on the group's stated policy, evaluated server-side; the not-permitted status is returned only to a confirmed member of that group. |
| T-04.1-48 | Denial of Service | concurrent joins overshooting the member cap | low | mitigate | The count and the insert happen in one transaction with the group row locked, so the cap cannot be raced. |
