<objective>
Build the group-only feed, its fixed cheer mechanic, and the export-consent gate that decides
whether a photo with other people in it may leave Ritham.

Purpose: GROUPEVENTS-04 requires a feed visible to the group and only the group — not public, not
discoverable, not indexed, with no generic shareable link and no tier above "this group." It also
enumerates what the feed must never show: pace, a time-based rank, a first-to-complete marker, a
denominator paired against completions, or anyone's precise location. Plan 04.1-10 already made
most of those impossible by giving them no column; this plan makes the last one impossible by
giving the feed response no field that could hold a total, and proves it with a structural gate.

The cheer mechanic is built new here. 04.1-RESEARCH.md Pitfall 1 is explicit that no cheer, kudos,
or reaction code exists anywhere in this codebase — only the product rule does — so this is the
first implementation, and it is deliberately count-free at the storage layer so no later surface
can compute a total from it.

Export consent lands here rather than with the certificate because a grant is a cross-user fact
that has to be checked from whichever device is exporting, and because the feed is where the photo
and the people visible in it are already known.

Output: the `internal/feed` package, migration `0006_feed`, and authenticated routes.
</objective>
