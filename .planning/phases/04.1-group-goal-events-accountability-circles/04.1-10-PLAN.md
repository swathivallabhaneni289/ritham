<objective>
Build Goal-Events, RSVP, and completion logging — a shared commitment with no clock, no
synchronized start, and no mechanism anywhere that could rank one person against another.

Purpose: GROUPEVENTS-02 is mostly a set of things that must not exist, and the safest way to
satisfy it is for those things to have nowhere to live. So the completion wire type has no
distance, pace, or route field; the completion table has no such column; and no query in this
package sorts or aggregates by anything but post time. `docs/group-events.md` §2 spells out what
each of those absences is defending against: Strava's Top Contributors board under a collective
goal, Racery's exposed contribution level, Apple Fitness's percentage score, and Peloton's live
per-class sort — showing a time and building a way to rank people by it are two different product
decisions, and Ritham makes only the first.

The pre-event headcount and the post-event completion view are deliberately separate routes. §2 is
explicit that this separation prevents the app from ever juxtaposing who said they were in against
who came out, which is what a denominator beside a completion count would do.

Output: the `internal/events` package, migration `0005_events`, a structural no-ranking gate, and
authenticated routes.
</objective>

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
