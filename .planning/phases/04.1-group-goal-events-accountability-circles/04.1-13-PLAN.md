<objective>
Build the Goal-Event surfaces a person interacts with before anyone has completed anything:
creating one, seeing the group's upcoming ones, and saying they are in.

Purpose: GROUPEVENTS-02 describes a shared, untimed commitment, and most of the design work here is
in what the screens refrain from doing. The create screen never calls the event a contest, in any
label, title, or empty state. The RSVP screen holds the headcount and nothing else holds it,
because `docs/group-events.md` §2 puts the pre-commitment signal on a separate screen from the
completion feed specifically so the app can never show who said they were in beside who came out.
And when an event's window passes, nothing changes visually for anyone: §2 requires the event to
close silently, with no expiry notice, no nudge, and no missed surface anywhere.

This plan also builds `ActivityTypeIcon`. 04.1-UI-SPEC.md corrects a mistaken claim that a
per-activity symbol mapping already exists — the codebase has only a binary cardio-versus-strength
glyph — so the mapping is authored here, from the UI-SPEC's own locked table.

Output: the `Social/GoalEvents/` directory, a shared activity icon, two new steps.
</objective>
